Welcome to the Open RBI Project

Open Engineering. Transparent Methods. Continuous Development. Free Software. Your Data. Your Security.

Open RBI applications are free to use, with no licence fees, subscriptions or per-user charges.

You can use the web-based calculators directly in your browser or download the applications to your own computer for local use. Your engineering files remain on your own system, under your control.

Unlike conventional Software as a Service (SaaS) platforms, Open RBI does not require you to upload engineering records to a central database or maintain an online account. No login or user ID is required, and Open RBI does not collect or retain the engineering data you enter.

This approach offers important security and confidentiality advantages. Sensitive plant information, inspection records and engineering assessments can be managed within your organisation's existing IT security and document control arrangements, without relying on third-party cloud storage.

For organisations operating in restricted or isolated environments, supported applications can also run offline.

Use the calculators online or download them. Keep your data on your own system. No subscriptions. No compulsory cloud storage.
