Welcome to the Open RBI Project

Open Engineering. Transparent Methods. Continuous Development.

Free software. Open standards. Independent engineering. Your data, your decisions.

The Open RBI Project is an independent engineering software initiative dedicated to making Risk-Based Inspection (RBI), corrosion engineering and asset integrity management more accessible, transparent and practical.

Our objective is straightforward: to provide engineers, asset owners, researchers and organisations with free engineering tools that support informed technical decisions without expensive software licences, complex database systems or dependence on a single commercial provider.

Open RBI brings engineering calculations, inspection planning, equipment information and technical documentation together in a flexible, user-controlled environment.

We believe engineering knowledge should be shared, calculation methods should be understandable, and improvements should emerge through continuous development, technical discussion and independent review.

Why Open RBI?

Engineering Transparency

Engineering decisions must be supported by sound technical reasoning. Open RBI promotes transparent calculation methods, identifiable assumptions and traceable results so engineers can understand how an assessment has been developed.

Free and Accessible Software

Open RBI is built on the principle that essential engineering tools should be accessible without licence fees, subscription charges or per-user costs.

Our aim is to lower the financial and technical barriers that prevent smaller organisations, independent engineers and developing industries from using structured asset integrity methods.

Recognised Engineering Standards

Open RBI draws upon established engineering methodologies, including API 580, API 581, ASME PCC-3 and other relevant engineering standards.

The project supports recognised engineering practices while distinguishing between published standard requirements, engineering interpretations and independently developed calculations.

Local Data Ownership

Your engineering information belongs to you.

Open RBI is designed around browser-based applications, locally controlled files and user-managed records. No login or user ID is required, and the project does not collect or retain entered engineering data.

You work in your browser and save your information locally, maintaining control over your assessments, inspection histories, equipment records and supporting documentation.

Continuous Design and Improvement

Engineering software should evolve alongside engineering knowledge.

Open RBI follows a continuous development approach in which calculations, software functions, workflows and documentation can be reviewed, challenged and improved.

Constructive technical feedback, independent verification and contributions from practising engineers are encouraged.

Engineering Tools for Real Industrial Applications

Open RBI is being developed as a collection of complementary engineering applications that can operate independently or form part of a wider asset integrity workflow.

These include risk-based inspection, corrosion and damage assessment, pressure equipment evaluation, inspection planning, engineering reporting and plant information management.

Fulcrum, the plant information organiser, provides a structured approach to managing equipment registers, technical documents, photographs, inspection histories and engineering records. It connects plant information with RBI assessment tools while leaving authoritative records under the asset owner's control.

The emphasis throughout the project is on practical engineering applications, understandable calculations and records that can be saved, reviewed and retained independently of the software.

A Different Approach to Engineering Software

Many engineering software systems depend on centralised databases, commercial licensing arrangements and ongoing subscription models.

Open RBI takes a different approach.

The software is a tool. The engineering judgement remains with the engineer.

Applications are designed to operate in ordinary web browsers, with options for local and offline use where supported. Engineering information can be retained within an organisation's existing file and document management systems.

The objective is to provide engineering capability without unnecessary software dependencies, licence restrictions or loss of control over technical information.

Collaboration and Continuous Development

Open RBI welcomes constructive participation from engineers, software developers, researchers, educators and industrial organisations.

We encourage technical discussion, independent testing, identification of calculation errors, suggestions for new capabilities and improvements to documentation.

Our long-term vision is a collaborative engineering environment in which software and technical methods improve through shared knowledge, independent scrutiny and practical industrial experience.

Open development encourages scrutiny, but professional engineering responsibility remains essential. Calculations and assessment methods require appropriate verification and validation before use in safety-critical applications.

Free Software and Professional Services

Open RBI separates access to engineering software from the provision of professional engineering services.

The objective is to make the software freely available without compulsory subscriptions, user licences or per-seat charges.

Organisations requiring additional assistance may obtain professional services such as training, implementation support, application customisation, engineering method development and integration with existing document management systems.

These services are separate from access to the free software.

Our intention is to develop a sustainable engineering ecosystem based on expertise, education and practical technical assistance rather than charging engineers simply to use their tools.

Getting Started

Explore the applications, examine the calculation methods and review the available training materials and technical documentation.

Use Fulcrum to organise plant information, investigate the RBI assessment tools and consider how Open RBI can complement your existing engineering workflows.

No account registration is required to use the available browser-based applications.

Feedback, technical discussion and suggestions for improvement are always welcome.

Engineering Responsibility

Open RBI provides engineering calculation, assessment and information management tools intended to support professional engineering work.

The software does not replace competent engineering judgement, independent verification, statutory requirements or formal engineering approval.

Users remain responsible for confirming that calculations, assumptions, input data and assessment methods are appropriate for their equipment and operating circumstances.

The Future of Open RBI

The future of engineering software should not be defined solely by expensive licences, closed calculation systems or restricted access to technical knowledge.

We believe there is another way: accessible software, transparent engineering methods, independent data ownership and continuous technical improvement.

Open RBI is an ongoing project, developed one application, one calculation and one improvement at a time.

We invite you to explore the tools, examine the methods, challenge the assumptions and contribute your experience.

OpenRBI. Free. Transparent. Local. Your decisions, your way.
